You may name your implementation as HTMLTrust, or in a way a reader would take
as an official release, only if it passes the published

and you should state the revision you passed against. There is no application,
no registry, and no fee. The suite is the gate and it is public.

Do not use the name for a modified specification. Fork if you want to, and
publish the result under a different name: two incompatible things called
HTMLTrust would make the name useless to readers, which is the outcome this
policy exists to prevent.
